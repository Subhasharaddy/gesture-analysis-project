CORE SYSTEM CAPABILITIES

Engineered For Real-World Assistive Impact

Combining cutting-edge computer vision with hardware actuation to create a robust, accessible bridge between the non-verbal and hearing worlds.

🖐️

MediaPipe 3D Landmark Tracking

High-precision 21-joint 3D spatial coordinate tracking per hand using Google MediaPipe Tasks API. Resilient to lighting changes, skin tone variations, and partial finger occlusions.

● Sub-millimeter Precision
🤝

Bimanual & Single-Hand Recognition

Seamlessly recognizes both complex two-handed prayer/roof postures (Namaste, Home) and single-handed communicative signs (Hello, Thank You, Good, Water).

● Dual-Hand Spatial Alignment
📐

73-Dimensional Invariant Vectors

Extracts distance-, scale-, translation-, and rotation-invariant geometric features and fingertip angles, ensuring reliable recognition regardless of distance to camera.

● Scale & Position Invariant
🗣️

Bilingual Speech & Kannada Unicode

Instant translation with localized voice synthesis (pyttsx3) and authentic Kannada script display (ಕನ್ನಡ) for inclusive regional and institutional communication.

● English & Kannada Audio
📟

Arduino IoT Hardware Bridge

Asynchronous non-blocking serial communication with Arduino Uno to transcribe gestures onto an LCD display and trigger emergency buzzers for HELP & STOP alerts.

● Serial 115200 Baud Protocol
📱

Smart Dual-Camera Architecture

Automatic sequential discovery and on-the-fly switching between laptop webcams and USB-connected Android smartphones (DroidCam/UVC) with zero frame freeze.

● Auto-Discovery & Hot-Switch
SYSTEM ARCHITECTURE

End-To-End Recognition Pipeline

How a physical hand gesture is transformed into real-time speech and hardware actuation in under 33 milliseconds.

01
📹

Video Feed Capture

Webcam or Android USB phone stream captures frames at 30 FPS. Buffer clamping eliminates latency.

02
🦴

3D Skeletal Tracking

MediaPipe Hand Landmarker isolates hands and extracts 21 precise 3D spatial joint coordinates per hand.

03
📐

Invariant Vectorization

Normalizes coordinates relative to the wrist and calculates 73 Euclidean distances and joint angles.

04
🧠

Random Forest AI

Trained ensemble model evaluates feature vectors with rolling temporal stability to eliminate flicker.

05
🔊

Bilingual & IoT Output

Speaks localized audio, updates Kannada Unicode, and transmits serial signals to Arduino LCD & Buzzer.

ABOUT THE RESEARCH INITIATIVE

Bridging Communication Gaps With Zero-Cost Hardware

Over 70 million individuals worldwide rely on sign language as their primary mode of communication. Traditional sensor gloves are fragile, costly, and cumbersome.

SignBridge AI provides an instantaneous, non-invasive solution that runs directly on ordinary laptops using standard RGB webcams or mobile phones. It bridges communication in hospitals, public services, banking desks, and academic halls without requiring an interpreter present.

💡 The Problem
Severe shortage of certified sign language interpreters causing isolation in emergency healthcare and education.
🎯 Our Solution
Real-time 3D landmark vision with bilingual speech output and IoT alerting, completely free of wearable hardware.
👥 Who Benefits
Deaf and hard-of-hearing students, healthcare professionals, customer service representatives, and public venues.
🚨 Emergency Ready
Instant priority detection for HELP and STOP gestures with physical Arduino buzzer and red alert flashing.
